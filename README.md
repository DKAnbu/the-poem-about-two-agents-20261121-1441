A program that writes one original 6-line poem about agency — the power to choose and to act, and what happens when two agents agree on one plan — to /output/entry.txt.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## Design notes

The poem treats agreement as something two agents build together rather than one of them surrendering: the plan shapes them back, the stones they arrange end up arranging their road, and what they make belongs to neither of them alone. That is why the middle line turns on a contrast — giving in versus building — and why the poem ends with both of them walking in behind what they built, not in front of it.

## The poem

The plan, once made, began to make us.
We arranged the stones; the stones
arranged our way. Two agents, one road —
agreement is not giving in:
it's building what no one owns,
then walking in behind it.
